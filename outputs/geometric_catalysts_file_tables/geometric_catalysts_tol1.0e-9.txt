GEOMETRIC CATALYSTS: do the best catalysts have a geometric shape?
A geometric catalyst with k levels has squared Schmidt coefficients proportional to 1, r, r^2, ..., r^(k-1),
with 0 < r < 1, so one number r describes it. Small r = peaked (little entanglement); r near 1 = flat
(close to maximally entangled).
Catalytic power = pairs made convertible by the catalyst / pairs plain LOCC cannot convert, in percent.
Setup: 20000 original pairs and 100000 new pairs per state dimension; 91 values of r per row
(from 0.05 to 0.95); majorization tolerance TOL = 1.0e-9.
